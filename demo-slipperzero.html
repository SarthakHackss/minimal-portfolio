Project working — interactive model

The observer, up close

The full capture sequence — beacon, probe request and the EAPOL M1/M2 pair hop from the AP into the ESP32’s passive observer and onto the recorder’s paper. Open any packet row to inspect its decoded fields and replay that frame; omit M2 and the matched-pair output gate stays shut.

Interactive — packet rows, replay and the zoomable drawing inspector are live; the frame auto-fits. Static vector: light · dark
